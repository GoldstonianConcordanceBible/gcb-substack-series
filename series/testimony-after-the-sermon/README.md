# The Testimony After the Sermon

This series is the personal witness layer of the Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB).

Each sermon receives one written reflection after it is preached.

The article is not a transcript.

It is the testimony after the sermon.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this series is to preserve the living formation behind the preaching.

Each article asks:

1. What was I trying to say?
2. What did I actually live while preaching it?
3. What did I get right?
4. What should I have corrected?
5. What did I pray about afterward?
6. What is God teaching me now?
7. How does this form me into the Fifth Gospel?

---

## Voice

This series is written in the voice of the preacher.

It should remain:

- Honest
- Personal
- Reflective
- Prayerful
- Corrective
- Testimonial

This is not Professor Enoch.

This is not institutional doctrine.

This is the field journal of a man being formed by the Word he is preaching.

---

## Formation Flow

Each article should align with:

Surrender → Abide in Christ → Mirror → Water → Fire

The sermon may begin in one phase, but the testimony should show the movement of formation.
