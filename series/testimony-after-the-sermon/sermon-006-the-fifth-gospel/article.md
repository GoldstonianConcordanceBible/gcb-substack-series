# After Sermon 006: The Fifth Gospel

## What God Showed Me After I Preached

Opening Scripture:

"You are the light of the world." — Matthew 5:14

---

## What I Was Trying to Say

This sermon was about becoming the Fifth Gospel.

Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John show Christ through Scripture.

The Fifth Gospel shows Christ through the life people are watching.

Not perfectly.

Not loudly.

Not for performance.

But truthfully.

I was trying to say that my life is already being read.

My children read it.

My family reads it.

Strangers read it.

People at The Rock read it.

Viewers online read it.

The question is not whether my life is preaching.

The question is what my life is preaching.

---

## What I Actually Lived While Preaching It

While I was preaching, I felt the responsibility of being watched.

Not in a prideful way.

In a sobering way.

If I say Christ changed me, then my life has to carry evidence of that change.

If I preach surrender, I have to surrender.

If I preach abiding, I have to abide.

If I preach mirror, I have to be corrected.

If I preach water, I have to be cleansed.

If I preach fire, I have to be refined.

The Fifth Gospel is not a brand.

It is accountability.

---

## What I Got Right

I believe I got the core message right.

The Fifth Gospel is not about replacing Scripture.

It is about becoming a living witness to Scripture.

The written Gospel remains the authority.

My life is only a testimony.

I also believe I got the family piece right.

The people closest to me should not see a different gospel than the people online.

The witness has to begin at home.

---

## What I Should Have Corrected

I should have made clearer that the Fifth Gospel is not about perfection.

It is about repentance, obedience, correction, and consistency.

I should have also emphasized that the Fifth Gospel is impossible without Christ.

No one becomes a living witness through willpower alone.

This requires surrender.

This requires abiding.

This requires the Holy Spirit.

---

## What I Prayed About Afterward

Lord,

Let my life reflect Christ.

Not my ego.

Not my ambition.

Not my platform.

Not my need to be seen.

Let my children see Christ in me.

Let my family see Christ in me.

Let strangers see Christ in me.

Let the sermon continue after the camera turns off.

Make my life a witness.

In Jesus’ name,

Amen.

---

## What God Is Teaching Me Now

God is teaching me that the Fifth Gospel is the fruit of the whole formation flow.

Surrender gives God my life.

Abiding keeps my life connected to Christ.

Mirror corrects my life.

Water cleanses my life.

Fire refines my life.

Then the life becomes readable.

Not because I made myself holy.

Because God formed me.

---

## The Fifth Gospel Reflection

The Fifth Gospel is my life after the sermon.

It is what I do after I say amen.

It is how I speak when I am tired.

It is how I love when I am corrected.

It is how I obey when no one is clapping.

That is where the real witness lives.

---

## Closing Witness

I preached the sermon once.

Now I have to live it daily.
